Time Compliexity (TC) :- Rate of Increase in Time with respect to Input Size
---------------------

Representation :- Big-O-Notation -> O(your time taken)
--------------

Rules for TC :- 
------------
  1. Always Caluclate TC in terms of Worst Case
  2. Avoid the Constant Values 
  3. Avoid Lower Bound
      eg :- TC = O(8N^6 + 3N^2 + 15) = O(N^6)

Types of TC :-
-----------
      1. Best Case
      2. Average Case
      3. Worst Case

Different Types of TC Caluclation :-
-----------------------------------
     1. Big-O-Notation O() :- Worst Case [Upper Bound]
     2. Theta Notation :- Average Case
     3. Omega Notation :- Best Case [Lower Bound]
